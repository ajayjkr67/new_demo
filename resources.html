Self-help

Apps & self-help tools

These tools can support your mental health between appointments or before you're ready to see a professional. They are not a substitute for clinical care.

Wysa
Indian AI-based mental health app with CBT and DBT-based exercises. Available on Android and iOS. Free tier available.
Headspace
Guided meditation and mindfulness. Evidence-based for stress, anxiety, and sleep. Subscription-based.
Daylio (Mood Journal)
Simple mood and activity tracker. Useful for identifying patterns in mood and triggers. Free, no account required.
Sleep Cycle
Sleep tracking and analysis. Helps identify sleep patterns and provides gentle wake-up alarm during light sleep phase.
MindShift CBT
Free CBT-based app for anxiety. Developed by Anxiety Canada. Practical tools for managing worry, panic, and social anxiety.
Calm
Meditation, sleep stories, and breathing exercises. Strong evidence base for stress and sleep improvement.
For families

Supporting a family member

Having a family member with a mental health condition is challenging. Here are some practical guidelines.

01
Listen without judgment or quick fixes
The most powerful thing you can do is make the person feel heard. Resist the urge to immediately problem-solve or offer reassurance. Just listen.
02
Learn about the condition
Understanding what your family member is experiencing reduces frustration, improves your ability to help, and helps you distinguish illness-behaviour from character.
03
Encourage treatment — don't force it
Express concern and offer to accompany them to an appointment. Ultimatums and pressure usually backfire. Consistent, gentle encouragement works better.
04
Take care of yourself
You cannot pour from an empty cup. Caregiver burnout is real and common. Seeking your own support is not selfish — it makes you a better support to your family member.
05
Attend family sessions if offered
Family education sessions can be arranged alongside the patient's treatment. These give you a structured way to understand the condition and learn how to help effectively.
