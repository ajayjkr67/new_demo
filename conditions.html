What We Treat

Conditions

Detailed information on mental health conditions treated at the clinic — what they are, how they present, and how they're treated.

Depression

Persistent low mood, loss of interest, fatigue, and cognitive changes. One of the most common and most treatable conditions in psychiatry.

Learn more

Anxiety Disorders

Excessive worry, panic attacks, phobias, and social anxiety. Anxiety disorders affect daily functioning but respond well to treatment.

Learn more

OCD

Obsessive Compulsive Disorder — intrusive thoughts and compulsive behaviours that can consume hours of the day. Highly treatable with the right approach.

Learn more

Bipolar Disorder

Episodes of mania or hypomania alternating with depression. Often misdiagnosed. Requires careful management and long-term support.

Learn more

ADHD

Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder — affecting focus, impulse control, and executive function in both children and adults.

Learn more

Dementia

Progressive cognitive decline affecting memory, reasoning, and daily functioning. Early diagnosis allows for better care planning and quality of life.

Learn more

Schizophrenia & Psychosis

Conditions involving hallucinations, delusions, and disorganised thinking. With proper treatment, many patients achieve stable, functional lives.

Learn more

PTSD

Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder — flashbacks, hypervigilance, and emotional numbing following traumatic events. Treatable with specialist care.

Learn more

Sleep Disorders

Insomnia, hypersomnia, and sleep problems related to psychiatric conditions. Often the first sign of an underlying mood or anxiety disorder.

Learn more
